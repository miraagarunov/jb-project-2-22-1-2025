About my project

In this project, I developed a website that provides information and reports from the world of virtual trading. The main goal was to create a user-friendly platform that consolidates up-to-date and relevant information for traders and enthusiasts in the field. Virtual trading has become a highly popular and dynamic area in recent years, so it was important for me to provide accessible and practical tools for understanding the market. On the page I created, users can find various information, such as:

  • Currency Status: Real-time data on the value of leading digital currencies.
  • Prices and History: Graphs and data displays showing the price fluctuations of currencies over time.

This project allows users to gain insights into the market, understand trends, and plan their next trading steps more intelligently.
